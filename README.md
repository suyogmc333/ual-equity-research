# United Airlines (UAL): Industry Analysis and Valuation

Equity research report on United Airlines Holdings, prepared for MGTF 412, Financial Statement Analysis, at the University of California, San Diego, Rady School of Management (March 2026).

**Rating: BUY · Price target $108.58 · Price $92.00 · Upside 18%**

## The argument

United's capex cycle has peaked, its free cash flow has swung from –$0.9B (2023) to +$3.4B (2025), and about $16B of debt is projected to be repaid by 2030. The market price doesn't yet reflect that shift.

## What's inside

- Industry analysis of U.S. airlines: market structure, demand, cost structure and macro risks
- Airline-specific accounting: air traffic liability, loyalty program revenue, lease capitalization under ASC 842
- Five-year three-statement projection (2026–2030E)
- FCFF DCF with a 9.0% WACC and 6.5x exit EV/EBITDA, plus a WACC × multiple sensitivity grid
